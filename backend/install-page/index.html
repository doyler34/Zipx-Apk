    Installing on Android

    1. Tap Download for Android above. The APK saves to your phone's Downloads.
    2. When it finishes, tap the download notification — or open your Files / My Files app, go to Downloads, and tap zipx-movies.apk.
    3. The first time only, your phone will say installs from this source are blocked. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source (this is normal for apps not on the Play Store), then press Back.
    4. Tap Install, wait a few seconds, then tap Open.
    5. If your phone shows a Play Protect / "unsafe app" warning, choose Install anyway — it flags anything installed outside the Play Store.

    Updating later: open the app and go to Profile → App Updates — it downloads and installs updates for you. Re-downloading from this page also still works and keeps your watchlist and settings either way.

    Installing on Windows

    1. Tap Download for Windows and run ZipX-Setup.exe when it finishes.
    2. If SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info → Run anyway — that's expected for a new app and is safe.
    3. Follow the installer. It adds a Start-menu shortcut (and a desktop icon if you tick the box).

    Updating later: open the app and go to Profile → App Updates — it downloads the new installer for you. Re-running the installer from this page also still works.
